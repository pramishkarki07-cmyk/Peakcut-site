Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

PeakCut is a personal video clipping tool run by its owner on their own computer. This policy explains what happens to information when the owner connects a TikTok, Instagram or YouTube account.

What we access

When you connect an account, we receive your basic profile information (such as your account name) and an access token. We use the token only to upload and post the clips you choose, using the permissions you approve.

Where it is stored

Tokens, settings and clips are stored on the computer where PeakCut runs. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose other than posting your own clips.

Third parties

Clips and captions are sent to the platform you choose (TikTok, Instagram or YouTube) so they can be posted. If you add an AI key, video transcripts may be sent to that AI provider to pick highlights.

Your choices

You can disconnect an account in PeakCut at any time, and you can remove PeakCut's access from your TikTok, Instagram or Google account settings. Deleting the PeakCut folder removes the stored data.
